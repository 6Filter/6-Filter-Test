# How the score works

## What the percent is

The percent is the share of Filter 1 to Filter 4 checks that are complete for this idea (17 checks: six in Filter 1, three in Filter 2, one in Filter 3, seven in Filter 4). Filters 5 and 6 are not in the percent. Filter 6 is validation only.

## Hard stops

A hard stop is applied first. A filter below a non-negotiable floor forces NO-GO, whatever the percent. A filter that has not run, or whose research failed, keeps the idea INCOMPLETE (not a GO/WATCH/NO-GO band) until Filter 1–4 finish — unless a floor failure already forced NO-GO.

## Bands

After the hard stops, complete ideas band as: GO above 80, WATCH 60 to 80, and NO-GO below 60. INCOMPLETE ideas still show the percent and checks-complete count, but are not banded.

## Where numbers come from

Research looks for a publicly cited Nielsen or Kantar figure first, then category engines, then a public proxy. A local Nielsen or Kantar row is used only when that category has been loaded. There is no live Nielsen or Kantar feed.

## Searches

Each research step may run up to 4 web searches. That budget is 1 when both a local Nielsen row and a local Kantar row match the exact category. The verdict sentence and the improvement list do not search. Submitting the same idea again reuses the saved research.

## Points to improve

The improvement list is only the checks still open on this idea. Each open check is an equal share of the remaining points, and the field owner is named.
